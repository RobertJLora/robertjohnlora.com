## Design Context

### Users
SEO professionals, developers, and curious visitors exploring Robert's portfolio site. Context: quick entertainment break, discovering what's possible with vanilla JS, "one more try" addiction loop. They're technically literate and appreciate craft.

### Brand Personality
Direct, playful, technically sophisticated. The site feels like a personal notebook (Apple Notes-inspired) that occasionally reveals deep technical skill through interactive builds.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Warm amber accent (#E8A836) on dark backgrounds – NOT neon-on-dark
- System fonts (SF Pro stack), no Google Fonts
- Games are self-contained dark-themed immersive experiences within the light/dark site shell
- Anti-references: glassmorphism, gradient text, cyan-on-dark, purple-to-blue gradients, thin colored accent strips
- Reference energy: Geometry Wars (visual density), Downwell (tight death loop), Vampire Survivors (escalating spectacle)

### Design Principles
1. Every frame is a screenshot – visual density through trails, particles, and layered effects
2. Every action feels heavy – freeze frames, shake, and sound on every meaningful event
3. The experience builds – music layers, visual intensity, and combo effects escalate with progress
4. Warm over cool – amber/gold/red palette, tinted neutrals, no pure black or white
5. Zero GC, 60fps always – object pools, typed arrays, no shadowBlur in hot paths
